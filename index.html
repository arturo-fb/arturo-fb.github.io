04 — Golf

Golf

Golf has been a core part of my life for over ten years, and I've played competitively for most of them. The game has taught me lessons in patience, discipline, and mental focus that transfer directly to how I approach analytical work.

What I love most is how closely it mirrors real life: a constant balance between strategy and execution, pressure and composure, and learning how to move forward after a setback. You cannot control the outcome of a single shot — only your process, your preparation, and your response.

Whether I'm playing alone or in tournaments, golf continues to shape my mindset and has helped me develop a calm, resilient approach to challenges on and off the course.
